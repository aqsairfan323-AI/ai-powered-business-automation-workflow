
# AI-Powered Business Automation Workflow

## Project Overview
An automated workflow designed for an online food business to streamline customer enquiry handling and improve business operations using Zapier.

## Problem Statement
Managing customer enquiries and repetitive business tasks manually can be time-consuming and may delay customer responses.

## Proposed Solution
A Zapier-based automation workflow that connects business tools to automate selected tasks, reduce manual effort, and improve enquiry management.

## Project Objectives
- Automate repetitive business processes.
- Improve customer enquiry handling.
- Reduce manual work.
- Enhance customer experience.
- Support business growth and retention.

## Tools Used
- Zapier – Workflow automation
- Miro – Workflow strategy and planning
- AI tools – Workflow design and content support
